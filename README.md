# Hotel Booking SQL Analysis

## Project Overview
SQL project analyzing hotel booking data to identify cancellation trends, customer behavior, revenue drivers, and booking seasonality.

The analysis was performed using SQLite and focuses on business questions commonly explored by Business Analysts and Data Analysts in the hospitality industry.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains hotel reservation information, including:

- Booking status
- Lead time
- Customer type
- Distribution channel
- Average Daily Rate (ADR)
- Length of stay
- Reservation details

Total Records Analyzed: 119,390 bookings

---

## Business Questions

### Query 1 – Total Bookings
Calculated the total number of reservations.

### Query 2A – Cancellation Overview
Compared canceled versus completed bookings.

### Query 2B – Cancellation Rate
Calculated the overall cancellation percentage.

### Query 3 – Revenue by Customer Type
Estimated revenue generated by each customer segment.

### Query 4 – Cancellation Rate by Distribution Channel
Identified which booking channels have the highest cancellation risk.

### Query 5 – Monthly Booking Volume
Analyzed booking seasonality.

### Query 6 – Lead Time Categories
Segmented bookings into Short, Medium, and Long Lead Time categories using CASE WHEN.

## Key Findings

- Overall cancellation rate was 37.04%.
- TA (Travel Agenty)/TO (Tour Operator) booking channels showed significantly higher cancellation rates than direct bookings.
- Transient customers generated the largest share of estimated revenue.
- Direct bookings had lower cancellation rates, indicating more reliable demand.
- Long lead-time reservations represented the largest booking segment.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Encourage direct bookings through loyalty programs and website incentives
- Implement cancellation risk monitoring for TA and TO channels.
- Develop retention strategies for high-value transient customers.
- Use lead-time segmentation to improve demand forecasting and inventory planning.
- Leverage seasonal demand patterns to optimize staffing and pricing strategies.
